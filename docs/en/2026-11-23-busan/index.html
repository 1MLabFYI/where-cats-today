🐾 Hermi's route

📍 Haeundae Blueline Park

Haeundae Blueline Park repurposes the old Donghae Nambu Line railway into a scenic coastal walk and ride in Busan. The Sky Capsule connecting Cheongsapo and Mipo offers breathtaking views of the ocean and sunset from an elevated track. Sunset and weekend time slots sell out quickly, so advance online booking is highly recommended. Plenty of sea-view cafés around Mipo and Cheongsapo stations let you relax before or after your ride. Keep a warm coat handy as coastal winter breezes can get chilly.

📍 Haeundae Light Festival

Every winter, the vast sandy expanse of Haeundae Beach transforms into a glowing wonderland of media art and light installations. The Haeundae Light Festival combines the rhythm of ocean waves with luminous sculptures, creating a magical night stroll. Light tunnels and photo spots extend from the beach up to Gunam-ro square, giving visitors plenty to see. Admission is free, with lights usually turned on from sunset until around 11:00 PM. Sea winds can be cold at night, so dress warmly with scarves and gloves.

📍 Gamcheon Culture Village

Famous for its pastel-colored houses stacked along steep hills, Gamcheon Culture Village is often dubbed the Machu Picchu of Korea. Murals, sculptures, and art installations line the narrow alleys, making it a joy to explore on foot. The statue of the Little Prince and the Desert Fox overlooking the village is the most iconic photo spot. Since local residents live here, visitors are kindly asked to keep noise levels down while exploring. Wear comfortable walking shoes as the village features many stairs and inclines.

📍 Busan's Winter Delights: Pork Soup & Seed Hotteok

No winter trip to Busan is complete without a steaming bowl of Dwaeji Gukbap (pork soup with rice). Rich pork bone broth served with tender pork slices and fresh chives instantly warms you up on a cold day. Historic gukbap joints can be found near Busan Station and in the bustling Seomyeon district. After your meal, head to BIFF Square in Nampo-dong for a hot, crispy seed hotteok pancake stuffed with crunchy nuts. It is the ultimate winter comfort food walk in Busan.

📍 Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market

In the evening, dive into the vibrant street food culture at Bupyeong Kkangtong Night Market in Nampo-dong. Starting at 7:30 PM, market stalls serve iconic local dishes like fried tofu pouch soup, spicy bibim noodles, and international snacks. Walking through the lively stalls offers a memorable culinary adventure for food lovers. Popular stalls often have lines, so take your time and stroll through at a relaxed pace. Bringing small cash or having mobile transfer ready will make ordering easier.
