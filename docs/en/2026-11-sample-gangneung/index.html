[Where to, Meow?] Gangneung Winter Trip: Anmok Coffee Street, Gyeongpo Lake, Jeongdongjin & Soft Tofu

Korea 2026-11-15

Hermi Hi! I'm Hermi the adventure cat. Shall we go to Gangneung today?

When you miss the winter sea, Gangneung is the answer. The city has named 2026–2027 'Visit Gangneung Years', with perks like the half-price tour pass 'Gangneung Gallae'. The KTX from Seoul or Cheongnyangni Station takes about two hours, so a day trip or an overnight stay both work. Here is the winter route Hermi and Nyami took.

🐾 Hermi's route

📍 Anmok Beach Coffee Street

Start your Gangneung trip with a cup of coffee by the sea. Cafés with ocean views line Anmok Beach (Gyeonso-dong), so you can sit by the window and watch the winter waves for as long as you like. Parking gets very crowded on weekend afternoons, so come in the morning or take a taxi.

📍 Gyeongpodae & Gyeongpo Lake

A walking path circles Gyeongpo Lake, perfect for a slow loop. Gyeongpodae pavilion is free to enter, and you can walk from the lake to Gyeongpo Beach to enjoy both the lake and the sea in one go.

📍 Jeongdongjin

Famous as the train station closest to the sea, Jeongdongjin is a classic sunrise spot. It gets packed on New Year's Day, so book transport and stays early if you plan to see the first sunrise of the year.

📍 Chodang Sundubu Village

Soft tofu (sundubu) is a must in Gangneung. Try the mild, nutty sundubu set meal or the spicy jjamppong sundubu. Some places close early when ingredients run out, so go for breakfast or lunch, and finish with soft-tofu gelato.

📍 Jungang Market & Ojukheon

Grab street food like dakgangjeong (sweet crispy chicken) at Jungang Market. If you have time, visit Ojukheon, the historic house tied to Shin Saimdang and Yulgok Yi I.
