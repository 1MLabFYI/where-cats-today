[Where to, Meow?] Tongyeong Winter Getaway: Dongpirang, Cable Car, Dpirang & Winter Oyster Feast

Korea 2026-11-22

Hermi Hi! I'm Hermi the adventure cat. Shall we go to Tongyeong today?

When winter winds start in November, Tongyeong becomes one of Korea's liveliest coastal towns. Known as the oyster capital producing the majority of Korea's fresh catch, it is the prime time to taste seasonal seafood. From sweeping archipelago views and quaint hill villages by day to an enchanted light park by night, here is Hermi and Nyami's complete Tongyeong winter travel guide.

🐾 Hermi's route

📍 Dongpirang Mural Village

Perched on a hillside overlooking Tongyeong Port, Dongpirang was once slated for demolition before transforming into a colorful arts sanctuary. Every winding alley is painted with vivid murals that make delightful photo backdrops. Climb to the hilltop Dongporu Pavilion for an unobstructed panorama of Gangguan Port and the downtown cityscape. Wear comfortable shoes for the sloped stairways, and remember to keep your voice down out of respect for local residents.

📍 Tongyeong Cable Car & Mireuksan

Carrying passengers up 461-meter Mount Mireuk, the Tongyeong Cable Car stretches nearly two kilometers, treating riders to panoramic archipelago views. From the upper station, a gentle ten-minute walk along wooden deck stairs brings you to the summit observation deck. On crisp winter days, you can spot Hansando, Geoje Island, and even Tsushima Island across the horizon. High winds can suspend operations, so check the official status online before you arrive.

📍 Dpirang Night Digital Park

When dusk falls, Nammangsan Sculpture Park transforms into Dpirang, a magical forest illuminated with digital art. It tells a charming folklore story that murals washed away from Dongpirang gather here at night to come alive. Along the 1.5-kilometer trail, visitors walk through shimmering projection-mapped installations and interactive light sculptures. The outdoor woodland route has uneven steps, so strollers are difficult; be sure to dress warmly and wear sneakers with good grip.

📍 Seasonal Tongyeong Oysters & Chungmu Gimbap

From November through February, Tongyeong oysters are at their plumper, sweetest peak. Specialized seafood eateries around the port serve multi-course feasts featuring raw oysters, golden oyster pancakes (guljeon), spicy oyster salad, and piping hot oyster stone-pot rice. For a quick local classic, head to Gangguan Port for authentic Chungmu gimbap—seaweed-wrapped bite-sized rice paired with spicy squid, fish cakes, and crunchy radish kimchi. Finish your food tour with traditional Tongyeong kkulppang, deep-fried honey-glazed pastries stuffed with red bean.

📍 Yi Sun-sin Park Coastal Walk

To savor the peaceful side of Tongyeong's coastline, visit Yi Sun-sin Park on the slopes of Mangilbong Peak. A commanding statue of Admiral Yi Sun-sin stands pointing toward Hansando Island, where historic naval battles unfolded. Gentle wooden boardwalks wind along pine groves and rugged shoreline cliffs. Winter mornings bring shimmering sea reflections, with scattered pavilions ideal for a quiet rest. On-site parking makes it a hassle-free stop for drivers.
