[Where to, Meow?] Kyoto Autumn Foliage Guide: Kiyomizu-dera, Tofukuji, Arashiyama & Yudofu

World 2026-11-19

Hermi Hi! I'm Hermi the adventure cat. Shall we go to Kyoto today?

From late November to early December, Kyoto turns into a breathtaking autumn wonderland draped in brilliant crimson and gold maples. From Kiyomizu-dera's illuminated night pagoda to the fiery foliage valley of Tofukuji, this is the most romantic time of year to visit. Taking the Kansai Airport Express 'Haruka' gets you to Kyoto Station in just 75 minutes, making it an easy getaway. Here is the ultimate Kyoto autumn itinerary curated by Hermi and Nyami.

🐾 Hermi's route

📍 Kiyomizu-dera Temple

Perched on the cliffs of Mount Otowa, Kiyomizu-dera is celebrated for its historic wooden main hall built entirely without nails. In autumn, the vast valley beneath the temple deck turns into a vibrant sea of fiery red maple leaves. The seasonal night illumination is truly unforgettable, with a soft blue beam cutting through the night sky and lighting up the glowing canopy. The evening queues can be substantial, so arriving slightly before lighting time around 5:30 PM or after the peak dinnertime rush is highly recommended.

📍 Tofukuji Temple

Regarded by locals as one of Kyoto's absolute best autumn spots, Tofukuji is renowned for its iconic Tsutenkyo covered bridge. Standing on this wooden walkway offers panoramic views over a dramatic ravine blanketed by more than two thousand crimson maple trees. The layers of deep red, burnt orange, and golden hues create an awe-inspiring gradient found nowhere else in the city. Because pedestrian traffic on the bridge peaks around midday, arriving right at the 8:30 AM gate opening ensures a peaceful and spectacular view.

📍 Arashiyama & Bamboo Grove

Located in western Kyoto, Arashiyama offers a serene countryside landscape framed by the gentle Hozu River and mountains dotted with fall foliage. After strolling across the historic wooden Togetsukyo Bridge, follow the path into the iconic Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (Chikurin). The rustling sound of towering green bamboo stalks creates a soothing contrast to the nearby autumn colors of Tenryu-ji's zen garden. Enjoying a hot cup of latte along the riverbank from % Arabica makes for a picture-perfect morning.

📍 Kyoto Yudofu & Uji Matcha Sweets

When the autumn air turns brisk, nothing warms you up better than Kyoto's signature simmered tofu hot pot, 'Yudofu'. Made with pure local spring water, the delicate tofu is gently simmered with kelp and served with seasoned dashi-soy sauce, grated ginger, and scallions. Afterward, treat yourself at a traditional tea salon to a multi-layered Uji matcha parfait loaded with intense green tea gelato, chewy shiratama dango, and sweet red bean paste.

📍 Nishiki Market

Boasting over 400 years of history, Nishiki Market is famously known as 'Kyoto's Kitchen', packing more than 130 food stalls along a narrow covered arcade. Here, you can sample freshly prepared local treats including dashi-infused rolled tamagoyaki omelets, grilled seafood skewers, and seasonal Kyoto pickles. Note that walking while eating is prohibited along the narrow street; be sure to stand and enjoy your snacks within the designated tasting area in front of each stall.
