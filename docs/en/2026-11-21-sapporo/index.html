[Where to, Meow?] Sapporo Winter Travel Guide: Odori Park Illuminations, Mt. Moiwa Night View, Otaru & Soup Curry

World 2026-11-21

Hermi Hi! I'm Hermi the adventure cat. Shall we go to Sapporo today?

From late November through December, as fresh snow begins to blanket the city, Sapporo turns into a fairytale winter wonderland. With the dazzling Sapporo White Illumination and the Munich Christmas Market in full swing at Odori Park, it is the premier season to experience winter holiday magic. Direct flights from Incheon or Busan take only about 2 hours and 40 minutes, making it an easy 3-to-4-day escape. Here is Hermi and Nyami's curated guide to Sapporo's top winter spots and cozy comfort eats.

🐾 Hermi's route

📍 Odori Park & Sapporo White Illumination

Stretching east-to-west through downtown Sapporo, Odori Park becomes the heart of the Sapporo White Illumination each winter, lit with hundreds of thousands of bulbs. Head up the Sapporo TV Tower observatory at 1-chome for an unobstructed panoramic view of the gleaming light corridor below. At 2-chome, the German Munich Christmas Market offers warm mulled wine, sweet roasted almonds, and charming holiday crafts. Temperatures drop quickly after dark, so bundle up with windproof gear and warm gloves.

📍 Mt. Moiwa Ropeway & Observation Deck

Recognized alongside Nagasaki and Kitakyushu as one of Japan's Top Three New Night Views, Mount Moiwa offers the most spectacular twilight panorama in Hokkaido. Take the aerial ropeway and the Morris Car mini cable car up to the 531-meter summit, where the snow-covered city grid glitters like scattered gems. The 'Bell of Happiness' at the observation terrace is a beloved photo stop for travelers. Arrive roughly thirty minutes before sunset to witness day fade into dazzling night, and bring pocket heat packs against the chilly summit breeze.

📍 Shiroi Koibito Park

Dedicated to Hokkaido's iconic 'Shiroi Koibito' cookies, this charming sweets theme park feels like a cozy European village. In winter, the courtyard gardens are adorned with whimsical winter illuminations and sparkling displays that make for delightful photos. Inside, visitors can watch cookies being crafted on the factory line or participate in sweet-making workshops. Located just a 7-minute walk from Miyanosawa Station on the Tozai Subway Line, it is effortlessly accessible from central Sapporo.

📍 Hokkaido Soup Curry & Sapporo Miso Ramen

When winter chill sets in, nothing warms you faster than Hokkaido's signature soup curry, loaded with fragrant spices, tender chicken leg, and crisp roasted vegetables. Squeeze a touch of fresh lemon onto your rice and dip spoonfuls into the steaming broth for ultimate comfort. Sapporo's rich miso ramen, crafted with deeply roasted soybean paste, chewy noodles, and tender chashu, is another absolute staple. To end your night like a true local, try 'Shime Parfait', Sapporo's unique tradition of wrapping up the evening with an elaborate dessert parfait.

📍 Otaru Canal Day Trip

Boarding a JR Rapid train from Sapporo Station gets you to the quaint port city of Otaru in roughly 35 minutes. At dusk, when 63 historic gas lamps flicker along the red-brick warehouse canal, the waterfront takes on an undeniably cinematic romance. After strolling the canal promenade, explore the renowned Otaru Music Box Museum and indulge in double fromage cheesecake at the LeTAO flagship store. Pavements along the coast can be icy, so take short, deliberate steps when walking.
